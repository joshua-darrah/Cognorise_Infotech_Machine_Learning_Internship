# Power BI Dashboard Specification — Customer Churn

Import `outputs/powerbi_customer_risk.csv` after the churn pipeline has run.

Recommended dashboard sections:

1. **KPI cards** — Customer count, churn rate, average churn probability, high-risk customer count.
2. **Risk segments** — Active vs At Risk vs High Risk.
3. **Churn trends** — churn rate by tenure group.
4. **Customer profile** — churn probability by contract, payment method, internet service and senior-citizen status.
5. **Risk table** — customer risk score/probability for retention prioritisation.
6. **Business actions** — identify high-risk groups for targeted retention campaigns.
